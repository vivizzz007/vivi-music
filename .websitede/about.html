About VIVI Music DE

VIVI Music DE is the desktop edition of VIVI Music — an elegant, expressive YouTube Music client. Built with Kotlin and Compose Multiplatform for Windows, Linux and macOS, it brings the same Material 3 interface to the desktop, with the bottom bar replaced by a collapsible sidebar and a dedicated device-sync screen.
