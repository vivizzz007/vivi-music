## Improvements & Fixes

### Improvements
- Upgraded YouTube video artwork and thumbnails to high definition (1280x720) across player and list views
- Added thumbnail shadow support for old player artwork
- Upgraded JioSaavn streaming integration with intelligent title normalization and scored candidate ranking, dramatically improving song matching accuracy
- Added in-memory LRU caching and HTTP connection pooling to make JioSaavn stream fetching and playback resolution ultra-fast
- Unlocked 320kbps audio streaming for Western and international catalog tracks on JioSaavn

### Bug Fixes
- Fixed an issue where song volume dropped or muted completely during playback with Audio Normalization enabled
- Fixed an issue where audio focus ducking (such as during keyboard typing or notifications) could leave volume stuck at a reduced level
- Fixed old player layout and controls positioning
- Fixed an issue where JioSaavn rejected valid matching songs due to minor album packaging differences (e.g., single releases vs album tracks)
- Fixed JioSaavn CDN 320kbps URL rewriting when stream links contain access tokens or query parameters
